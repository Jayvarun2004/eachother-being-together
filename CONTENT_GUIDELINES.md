# Content Guidelines

This document defines what is and isn't allowed in card contributions for each level.

## The Three Levels

### 🌸 Wholesome
**Audience:** All ages, all comfort levels.

**Allowed:**
- Funny, lighthearted questions about everyday life
- Questions about hobbies, dreams, memories, and personality
- Dares that are silly, creative, or involve safe physical activity
- Dares safe to perform over a video call

**Not allowed:**
- Any romantic or sexual content
- Anything that could be embarrassing in front of strangers
- Physical dares that could cause injury

**Examples of good Wholesome cards:**
- Truth: *"What is your biggest irrational fear?"*
- Dare: *"Do your best impression of your partner for 30 seconds"*
- NHIE: *"I have eaten an entire pizza by myself"*
- WYR: *"Would you rather be able to fly or be invisible?"*

---

### 💋 Flirty
**Audience:** Couples and close friends who are comfortable with romantic topics.

**Allowed:**
- Romantic questions about feelings, attraction, and relationships
- Questions about relationship milestones and preferences
- Dares involving non-sexual physical affection (hand-holding, slow dancing, shoulder massages)
- Questions that acknowledge romantic attraction

**Not allowed:**
- Sexually explicit content
- Questions about past sexual experiences
- Dares involving undressing or sexual acts
- Content that would make someone uncomfortable in a wholesome setting

**Examples of good Flirty cards:**
- Truth: *"What was the moment you knew you had feelings for me?"*
- Dare: *"Give your partner a 60-second shoulder massage"*
- NHIE: *"I have kissed someone in the rain"*
- WYR: *"Would you rather always be the big spoon or always be the little spoon?"*

---

### 🔥 Spicy
**Audience:** Adult couples (18+) who have confirmed consent.

**Allowed:**
- Suggestive romantic questions that imply intimacy without being explicit
- Questions about attraction, desire, and relationship dynamics
- Dares that are romantic and mildly physical but tasteful
- Content that is daring, cheeky, and adult-flavoured

**Not allowed (absolute rules):**
- ❌ Sexually explicit descriptions or graphic content
- ❌ Content that is degrading, humiliating, or disrespectful
- ❌ Dares that could be coercive or non-consensual
- ❌ Content involving or targeting third parties
- ❌ Anything that could cause physical or emotional harm
- ❌ Content that promotes unsafe behaviour

**Examples of good Spicy cards:**
- Truth: *"What's something you've always wanted to confess to your partner?"*
- Dare: *"Whisper something you've always wanted to say but haven't"*
- NHIE: *"I have had a romantic dream so good I was disappointed to wake up"*
- WYR: *"Would you rather be irresistible to your partner physically or mentally?"*

**The Spicy boundary — in plain English:**
Spicy cards should feel like something from a grown-up couple's game night — romantic tension, vulnerability, and adult themes — not an adult film or a harassment scenario. When in doubt, ask: *would this card make someone feel respected and safe?* If the answer is maybe, tone it down.

---

## Universal Rules (All Levels)

1. **No third-party shaming.** Cards must not involve mocking or shaming other people.
2. **No explicit content.** Even at Spicy, we do not allow graphic sexual language.
3. **No unsafe dares.** Dares must be safe to perform. No fire, no heights, no alcohol consumption required, no driving.
4. **No discrimination.** Cards must not target, mock, or exclude anyone based on gender, sexuality, race, body, religion, or any other characteristic.
5. **No duplicate cards.** Check existing cards before submitting. `npm run validate-cards` catches duplicates.
6. **Length limit.** Card text must be 10–300 characters.

---

If you're unsure whether a card is appropriate, open an issue and ask. We'd rather discuss it than reject a well-intentioned contribution.
